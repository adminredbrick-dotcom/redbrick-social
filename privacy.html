What this app does

"Red Brick Social Poster" is an internal tool used only by Red Brick Lettings to publish the company's own scheduled posts to the company's own Facebook Page and Instagram account. It is not offered to the public and nobody else can log in to it.

What data it uses

It uses an access token for the Red Brick Lettings Facebook Page and its linked Instagram business account, the Page and account IDs, and the images and captions the company has approved for publishing. It does not collect, read or store personal data about anyone else, and it does not read messages, comments or followers.

Where it is kept

The access token is stored as an encrypted secret in the company's private GitHub settings. Images and captions are published content. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Data deletion

The app holds no data about users other than the company itself. To remove the app's access, remove "Red Brick Social Poster" under Facebook Settings, Business integrations, or email info@redbrick-lettings.co.uk and we will delete the stored token within 7 days.
